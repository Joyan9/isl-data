How we calculate

We use the match data extracted from FotMob. xG is the quality of chances, not a prediction of the score. For Fair result?, each non-own-goal shot with xG is an independent Bernoulli chance and the team goal distributions are convolved exactly; own goals have no xG and are excluded. This is a small, non-commercial fan project: roast the scoreline, never the people.
